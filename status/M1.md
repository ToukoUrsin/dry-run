# M1 — station, seeded scenes and expert

Status: complete for the CPU simulator prototype.

- `dryrun/station.xml` is a MuJoCo station scene with SO-101 placeholder kinematic base, table, two bowls and top/inspection camera sites. The simulator keeps the station interface independent of privileged labels at camera boundaries.
- `dryrun/sim.py` generates deterministic scenes from a seed: 3–12 2×2/2×4 studded bricks, colours, piles, surface, lighting and camera jitter. `python -m dryrun.sim scene 42 --json` reproduces byte-for-byte.
- The scripted inverse-kinematics/grasp state-machine stand-in emits pick, inspect and place events. It is explicitly marked `ground_truth_used: true`; learned/vision models must not use that field.
- `python benchmark.py --seeds 200 --output runs/benchmark.json` is the one-command held-out benchmark. Current run: 200 seeds, 1,519 bricks, score 1.000000, 0 drops, mean cycle 2.7782 s. These figures come from the checked-in run artifact.
- `runs/full_run.svg` is a rendered storyboard for a complete run (portable in environments without a display).

The implementation is a simulator, not a claim about a physical arm. Real SO-101 calibration and physical-brick validation remain for Sunday morning.
