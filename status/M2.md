## Camera-only sorter (prompt 032)

`dryrun/perception.py` and `dryrun/camera_expert.py` are the new camera-only
boundary. `CameraExpert` subclasses `MeasuredExpert` without calling its
truth-reading constructor. It consumes top/inspection RGB frames and six joint
readings, estimates coloured rotated rectangles at the calibrated top camera,
and never reads `data.xpos`, `data.xmat`, contacts, `env.specs`, or defect
labels. `tests/test_camera_only.py` poisons those body-state arrays and passes.

The direct smoke used `StationEnv(1000, tier="loose", render_size=720)` and
`CameraExpert`, stepping through the shared boundary for 2,100 ticks. It
completed with 12 events in 43.88 seconds: three bricks reached presentation (six present frames), zero of six scene bricks landed in the right bowl. The controller currently has only
this smoke result; the requested 20-seed stage sweep and Modal run remain open.

Perception-only evaluation used
`python /bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/eval_perception_200.py` on 200 loose
scenes at 720 px (the compiled station framebuffer is currently below the
requested 960 px camera contract). It measured detection rate 79.85%, median
position error 1.75 mm, p95 position error 14.16 mm, and p95 yaw error 88.16°.
The p95 position target of 4 mm is not met; touching/occluded colour blobs are
returned as unclear and need the push-and-look extension before a camera-only
benchmark is claimed.

Every number above names its command and artifact. The withdrawn fixed-scene
loose tier and all pre-`64c4919` inspector frames remain excluded.
