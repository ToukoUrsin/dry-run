## Camera-only sorter (prompt 032)

`dryrun/perception.py` and `dryrun/camera_expert.py` are the new camera-only
boundary. `CameraExpert` subclasses `MeasuredExpert` without calling its
truth-reading constructor. It consumes top/inspection RGB frames and six joint
readings, estimates coloured rotated rectangles at the calibrated top camera,
and never reads `data.xpos`, `data.xmat`, contacts, `env.specs`, or defect
labels. `tests/test_camera_only.py` poisons those body-state arrays and passes.

The direct smoke used `StationEnv(1000, tier="loose", render_size=720)` and
`CameraExpert`, stepping through the shared boundary for 2,100 ticks. It
completed with 12 events in 43.88 seconds: three bricks reached presentation (six present frames), zero of six scene bricks landed in the right bowl.

The required Modal stage sweep used the same public rollout boundary with an
ephemeral `offheight=960` station XML, `render_size=960`, and `MUJOCO_GL=osmesa`.
The aggregate is `/bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/camera_stage_sweep_20.json`; its
runtime common ancestor is commit `0cb10d1` (including the perception fix
`2a3ffca`). It covers 20 loose seeds / 100 expected bricks: 19 completed (97
bricks) and seed 14 explicitly timed out after more than three minutes. Among
completed bricks, stage totals are seen=10, reached=10, gripped=7,
presented=7, right bowl=2, false accepts=0, false rejects=5. `gripped` is the
closed-jaw joint proxy and all event brick ids are opaque camera tokens; truth
is consulted only after control for scoring. The timeout is retained as a row,
not filled with invented stages.

Perception-only evaluation used
`python /bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/eval_perception_200.py` on 200 loose

## Camera yaw correction (prompt 044)

The original 200-scene perception artifact reported yaw median `35.265°` and
p95 `88.155°` because it compared every footprint with a half-turn period and
used a PCA axis. Commit `233cc77` now fits the segmented blob with a
minimum-area rectangle, reflects image y-down into world yaw, and evaluates a
2x2 with its quarter-turn symmetry (2x4 remains half-turn). The corrected
command is `python /bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/eval_perception_200.py`; its
artifact `/bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/perception_eval_200.json` measures 883
matched bricks across 200 scenes, yaw median `0.192°`, p95 `18.335°`, and
detection rate `88.03%`. The separate variant audit
`/bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/yaw_audit_044.json` reports raw size-aware median
`0.183°` and confirms the sign and 90-degree transforms are worse. The planner
passes `yaw + π/2` to the jaw, which is the short axis for 2x4 and is covered by
all four equivalent sides for 2x2.

The post-fix Modal rerun used the public rollout API in five isolated batches
with `--tier loose --max-steps 2100 --render-size 960`; the merge command and
rows are in `/bulk3/runs/dryrun/m2_rebuild/camera_stage_sweep_20_yaw.json`.

false accepts=0, false rejects=5. The right-bowl count is therefore `2/97`;
the yaw measurement is fixed even though later grasp/perception limits leave
