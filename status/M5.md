## Nemotron curriculum machinery (expert tuning gate)

attempt) into `/bulk3/runs/dryrun/m5/nemotron_accuracy_v4_diagnoses.jsonl`

```text

  --size 4 --seed 7 --measured-expert \
  --out /bulk3/runs/dryrun/m5/curriculum_expert_tuning_v2-20261115-size4.json
```

Source commit at command start: `bd6fc4328d9fbb4d33716cab92a30fc233fcb6f4`.

the Nemotron-suggested scenes sorted 9/24 bricks (`0.375000`), while the
