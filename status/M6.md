# M6 — real MuJoCo replay page live

The public page at https://dryrun-junction-2026.fly.dev/ now opens on the
compiled MuJoCo station scene. The GLB contains the SO-101 links, black motor
parts, studded bricks, and ring bowls; the viewer applies recorded body poses
from the replay, starts automatically at 1.5×, loops, and keeps both recorded
camera videos in sync. `present` and `verdict` events show the inspection frame
card with GOOD or DEFECTIVE and its score. The first screen headline is the
measured tier result: loose 382/382, touching 217/494, and dropped pile 31/386
from benchmark commit `64c4919`.

Source/deployment commit for this proof: `8a49a88` (page and server), with the
scene exporter in `ef18ecc`. The uploaded GLB is 1,197,912 bytes and 64,327
triangles, under the viewer budget. The mounted featured replay is seed 1001
from post-main commit `40bef5f`, with 24 events and 1,112 recorded frames.
