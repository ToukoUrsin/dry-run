Built with Codex

A short record of how this page was made and what the simulator can prove.

What is simulated: the official SO-101 MJCF, rigid Duplo-shaped bricks with studs and seeded defects, two bowls, both cameras, contact physics, and the scripted expert.

What is not simulated: a physical arm, real camera noise, and a policy score unless its checkpoint was actually benchmarked. The expert may use simulator truth; a learned policy may not.

Build record: five parallel Codex work streams used goal mode and ultra effort, with models assigned by job. The live timeline below renders the saved log; reviews and what they caught remain in CODEX.md. The cast label is truthful about whether the local recording is available.
